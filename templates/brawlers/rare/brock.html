Brock

"The box goes boom!" A long-range rocket specialist built to completely control map choke points and smash open enemy cover.

Offence 5 / 5
Defence 4 / 5
Utility 3 / 5
Range Very Long (9)

Main Attack: Rockin' Rocket

Brock fires a rocket missile towards his enemy dealing 2320 damage, and after setting off leaves a 2.9s flame dealing 688 damage.

Projectile Speed Slow (2700 units)
Reload Speed Normal (1.95s seconds)

Super: Rocket Rain

Brock fires a volley of 9 rockets across a large area, dealing 2,080 damage each while breaking nearby walls and bushes. This makes him amazing at crowd control and dealing massive area damage!

Wall Destruction Yes
Total Rockets 9 Projectiles (13 with Star Power)

Hypercharge: Rocket Barrage

When activated, Brock fire 4 waves of 7 rockets, each dealing 840 damage during his Hypercharge. With his More Rockets star power, it fires 5 waves instead of 4!

Stat Boosts +20% Speed, +25% Damage, +5% Shield

Health Points 6000

Movement Profile Normal

How to Master Brock

1
Firing Through Walls

Brock's main attack leaves a 1.5-tile splash radius giving you the opportunity to fire through walls when they are walking along the wall!

2
Maximise your Super

You can use brock super to quickly kill opponents when they are together, and running with your super a direction will shift the barrage towards it.

3
Slow down with Main Attack

Brock has one of the slowest reload speeds so it's a good idea to use up your attacks slowly to maximise what you have instead of spamming.

4
As enemies retreat, attack.

When your enemies are retreating, you can actively push towards the center to take control, and have more opportunities to attack.

Competitive Meta Build

Best Gadget
Rocket Lace

This allows you to quickly evade enemies in situations where you may be stunned or knocked down which is especially useful in gamemodes like Gem Grab.

Best Star Power
More Rockets or Rocket No. 4

More rockets is useful in specific gamemodes such as Heist, however Rocket No. 4 is more versatile across more gamemodes allowing you to save more rockets.

Recommended Gears
Shield + Damage

The shield provides more health to compensate Brock's lower health, and damage allows you to play more risky moves in scenarios where each rocket counts.

Remember: There is no such thing as a perfect guide and you should continue to adapt to your own experience and playstyle.
